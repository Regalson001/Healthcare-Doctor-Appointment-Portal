# MedPulse — Healthcare & Doctor Appointment Portal

> A modern, responsive, and feature-rich clinic management and patient appointment portal built with HTML5, Tailwind CSS, and Vanilla JavaScript. Designed to demonstrate practical business solutions, clean form validation, and accessible medical service scheduling.



---

## 🚀 Key Features

* **Clinic Departments & Services:** Showcases specialized medical units including Cardiology, Neurology, Orthopedics, and Pediatrics.
* **Specialist Physician Profiles:** Filter doctors dynamically by medical specialty or search by physician name with real-time grid updates.
* **Interactive Appointment Booking:** Secure scheduling form with patient registration fields, doctor selection dropdowns, preferred time slots, and dynamic success confirmation banners.
* **Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ):** Accordion-style layout addressing common patient policies and insurance inquiries.
* **Clinic Location & Hours:** Contact details, Google Maps-ready address layout, and operating schedules.
* **Modern Glassmorphic UI:** Built with dark-mode slate aesthetics, translucent panel styling, smooth hover micro-interactions, and custom scrollbars.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

* **Markup:** HTML5 (Semantic Structure)
* **Styling:** Tailwind CSS (Utility classes) + Custom CSS (`styles.css` glassmorphic styling)
* **Icons & Fonts:** FontAwesome 6.4.0 & Plus Jakarta Sans (Google Fonts)
* **State & Logic:** Vanilla JavaScript (Dynamic filtering, doctor selection binding, and form validation)
